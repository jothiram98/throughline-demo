# A client walkthrough of Throughline Phase 1

1. **Overview:** the shared customer picture across Sthalam, Woodinc and Kreate by Kraft.
2. **Data imports:** choose a business, use the sample customer report, map columns, review three new customers, one existing customer and one invalid row, then import.
3. **Customers:** find a customer, read their source/history, add a note and record sample permission evidence.
4. **Campaigns → Create campaign:** name the campaign, choose the business, inspect an audience and its excluded contacts, personalize an approved template, choose a sample time, review and schedule.
5. **Campaign details:** run the sample campaign, inspect recipients and view delivery/reply reporting. Open a completed seeded campaign to see matched purchases/bookings and returning customers.
6. **Shared inbox:** choose a customer, assign an owner, write a sample reply, save a team note and record a booking request.
7. **Ad performance:** compare Meta/Google reports and open a campaign to see the difference between platform metrics and captured leads.
8. **Lead pipeline:** simulate a native form lead, inspect its campaign/form IDs, assign it and create a follow-up. The new customer has unknown marketing permission.
9. **Orders & bookings:** import the sample outcome report to see a matched customer, an unmatched guest bill and an invalid amount; only valid rows are imported.
10. **Connections / Team:** review sample sender accounts, source reports and business-unit access.

All of these actions remain in the browser and use sample data. No provider accounts are connected.
